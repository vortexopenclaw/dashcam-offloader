What this estimate includes

All channels in the sampled configuration record together. Their bitrates are added, not their recording times. Presets are sampled configurations, not maximum-quality specifications or a promise for every firmware version.

Driving only: parking footage is not mixed into the bitrate. On a shared card, parking and protected files can reduce the driving history retained. On a partitioned card, use the normal-driving allocation even if parking recording is disabled.

Ranges reflect the recorded bitrate ranges, not statistical confidence intervals. Scene complexity and camera settings can change actual results. Card compatibility is not checked.

Formula: hours = card GB × driving share × (1 − headroom) ÷ (total Mbps × 0.45). GB and TB use the decimal units printed on cards.
